Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

BA (Badminton Analysis, “the Service”) is a small, invitation-only website at ba.llmat.dev, run by an individual (“the operator”, “we”). This policy explains what information the Service collects, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account information. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, your name and the address of your profile picture. We request only the basic openid, email and profile scopes. We never receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Account records. Your role (administrator or annotator), whether your account is active, when it was created and when you last signed in.
  • Your labeling work. The tasks you claim and submit, and every label you save, together with your email address and the time.
  • Recordings and results. Match recordings uploaded by administrators, images and short clips cut from them for labeling, and analysis results. These may show identifiable people on court.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider processes IP addresses and standard request logs in order to deliver and protect the site.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and decide what you are allowed to see.
  • To hand out labeling tasks so that two people do not label the same footage, and to record who labeled what.
  • To show administrators each member’s progress and to review the quality of labels.
  • To analyse recordings and to evaluate and train the models that do the analysis.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use Google user data for advertising or for any purpose other than the ones listed above.

3. Cookies

The Service uses only the cookies it needs to work: a sign-in session cookie (kept for up to 30 days) and a short-lived cookie used during Google sign-in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

4. Who can see your information

  • Administrators can see members’ names, email addresses, sign-in times and labeling activity.
  • Exports. Labels can be exported as files that include the annotator’s email address. These files are used by the operator for model evaluation and training.
  • Service providers. The site, its database and its file storage run on Cloudflare. Google provides sign-in. Recordings are processed on a computer controlled by the operator. To write match reports, a text summary of match statistics (not the video, and not member account information) is sent to AI models hosted on Microsoft Azure.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days, or when you sign out.
  • Original recordings are removed from cloud storage once they have been transferred for processing; recent results and labeling material stay in the cloud while they are in use.
  • Account records and labels are kept while the project is active. If your account is removed, the labels you made are kept and remain attributed to your email address unless you ask for that attribution to be removed.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the Service at any time and revoke its access in your Google account settings. You can ask the administrator for a copy of your account information, to correct it, to delete your account, or to remove your email address from labels you made.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Session tokens are stored only in hashed form, and access to recordings is limited to administrators and to annotators working on a task for that recording. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: contact the administrator who invited you.
